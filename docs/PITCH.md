# CreatorTrust — one-page pitch

**CreatorTrust by Servalia** is a publishing and marketing Mind for authors. It turns one manuscript into governed assets for Amazon KDP, IngramSpark, email, social, podcast, and video — without taking control away from the author.

**Pitch:** CreatorTrust is a persistent publishing Mind that turns one manuscript into governed publishing and marketing assets across KDP, IngramSpark, email, social, podcast, and reader channels, without taking control away from the author.

Minds does the work. Servalia makes it trustworthy. The author remains the operator.

## Why it wins this track

Generic writers produce volume. They forget which claims the author rejected, which rights were declared, and which edition is actually going to KDP. CreatorTrust binds one Mind to one author and runs: manuscript → repurpose → govern → approve → package → launch → learn.

## Demo loop

Mara Ellison has a manuscript. CreatorTrust turns it into five source-linked launch assets, catches an unsupported sales guarantee, learns her correction, prepares KDP and IngramSpark packages without auto-publishing, and leaves an audit trail. Script: `docs/DEMO-VIDEO.md`.

## Pricing hypothesis (illustrative)

- Self-serve: subscription per author or per active book, Mind usage included.
- Studio: small presses running several author Minds under one tenant later.
