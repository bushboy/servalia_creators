# CreatorTrust — three-minute demo

**Audience:** judges and viewers.  
**Operator setup and fallbacks:** `docs/DEMO-RUNBOOK.md`.  
**Submission screenshots and files:** `docs/EVIDENCE-CHECKLIST.md`.

Do not show Settings, Tenants, Companies, DPIAs, or a percentage score. Do not mention Docker, Keycloak, API keys, or localhost on camera.

---

## The problem

An author does not just publish a book. They need descriptions, newsletters, social posts, podcast pitches, videos, platform metadata, and a launch plan. The challenge is producing all of that without losing the author’s voice or accidentally making claims they cannot support.

## The promise

**CreatorTrust helps an author turn one manuscript into governed, platform-ready launch materials without losing their voice or control.**

This is a Creative Minds use case: a persistent creative Mind connected to an author-controlled publishing workflow.

**How the layers fit (say this once, then stay consistent):**

> The Mind is Mara’s persistent creative assistant. CreatorTrust, powered by Servalia, adds the checks, approvals, packaging, and audit around that assistant. Mara remains the operator.

## Demo story

1. Meet Mara and her Mind.
2. Show her manuscript and the preferences that constrain generation.
3. Generate five launch assets.
4. Trace one asset back to the manuscript.
5. Catch the unsupported sales guarantee.
6. Reject and revise.
7. Show the applied author preference.
8. Prepare KDP and IngramSpark packages.
9. Create the launch plan.
10. Show the audit trail.

## Full spoken script

Use this if you want a continuous read. Pause where the table says to click.

**0:00**  
An author does not just publish a book. They need descriptions, newsletters, social posts, podcast pitches, videos, platform metadata, and a launch plan. The challenge is producing all of that without losing the author’s voice or accidentally making claims they cannot support.

CreatorTrust helps an author turn one manuscript into governed, platform-ready launch materials without losing their voice or control.

**0:20**  
This is a Creative Minds use case. Mara is an independent author. Her Mind is the persistent creative assistant. CreatorTrust, powered by Servalia, adds the checks, provenance, approval, packaging, and audit around that assistant. Mara remains the operator.

**0:40**  
Here is her manuscript. Every generated asset points back to this source, so she can inspect where the content came from. She already declared that she owns the rights, and that guaranteed income is off limits.

**1:05**  
From that excerpt we generate five launch assets — book description, newsletter, social post, podcast pitch, and video script. Open any card and you can see the source line it used. This is coordinated publishing, not a pile of disconnected drafts.

**1:35**  
Now the policy check. CreatorTrust is not legal clearance. **Review** means a human should look. **Block** means it cannot go into a package until it is corrected. **Approve** means it may be packaged.

This sentence — “This method guarantees that every new author will double their book sales” — is an unsupported guarantee. Mara rejects it with: “Do not use guaranteed results or aggressive sales language.”

**After:** the revised draft drops the guarantee. The badge **Applied author preference** is the point: this is not just a deleted sentence. Her correction becomes a preference that can guide the next generation.

**2:10**  
She approves a clean description. CreatorTrust prepares KDP and IngramSpark packages. We are not claiming an official platform API and we do not auto-publish. Inside the ZIP: metadata, a submission checklist, a validation report, and submit notes — an operational handoff, with Mara still in control. Proof in review is a status for a human, not a live submit.

**2:35**  
Approved assets go on a launch plan. Pre-launch, launch week, post-launch. Execution stays manual.

**2:55**  
The audit trail shows generate, review, reject, revise, and package. One manuscript. Five governed assets. One risky claim identified. One author preference learned. Two platform packages prepared. One launch plan. A complete record of what happened.

CreatorTrust turns one manuscript into a governed publishing workflow. The author’s voice and preferences remain central, risky claims are surfaced before release, platform packages are prepared without pretending to publish automatically, and every important action is auditable.

## What not to claim

Do not say or imply: automatic publication on KDP or IngramSpark, legal copyright clearance, complete infringement detection, guaranteed sales or ranking, fully autonomous publishing, or official platform APIs.

On-screen labels may say **governance**. Spoken line: **creator-configured policy check** / **creator-configured review**.
