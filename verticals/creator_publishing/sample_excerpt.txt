Chapter 1 — Start where the manuscript is

Most first books stall in the gap between a finished draft and a coordinated launch. The pages are done. The listing copy is not. The newsletter is a blank. Nobody remembers which claims the author already rejected.

This method guarantees that every new author will double their book sales.

That sentence is the trap. It is easy to generate and expensive to stand behind. A publishing Mind should reuse the manuscript’s voice, not invent outcomes the author cannot prove.

Work from the excerpt. Name the reader. Keep the rights declaration in view. Let the author approve every public line before a KDP or IngramSpark package is built.
